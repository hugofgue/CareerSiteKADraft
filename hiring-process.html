Our Hiring Process

A clear, transparent journey from application to offer.

What to Expect

Hiring at K&A is designed to be a two-way conversation. We want to understand your experience, goals, and strengths, and we want you to get a real feel for our people, projects, and culture. Here's exactly what to expect, step by step.

Five Steps to Joining K&A

1

Create Your Profile

Join our Global Talent Community. Quick and simple. Once you're in, recruiters can reach out when a role matches your background.

2

Apply

Upload your CV and supporting documents. Include the job reference number if listed, so we can route your application correctly.

3

Sourcing and Candidate Selection

Our recruiters review every application carefully.

4

Interview

Conversations with our internal teams: HR, hiring manager, and technical panels. When the role requires it, we also coordinate a client interview.

5

Offer & Onboarding

If selected, you'll receive a formal offer followed by a smooth onboarding process: paperwork, induction, and your first project team.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the main stages of the process?

The process moves through five stages: profile creation, formal application, sourcing and candidate selection, interviews with our internal teams (and a client interview where the role requires it), and offer & onboarding. Each stage depends on the role, the number of applicants, and the different steps involved. We focus on a thorough and fair evaluation for every candidate.

Can I apply to multiple roles?

Yes. We encourage you to apply to any role where your background fits. If several positions match, our recruiters will help you focus on the best fit.

Do I need to be in the same city as the role?

Not always. For many roles we support relocation across our offices in Dubai, Riyadh, Abu Dhabi, and beyond. Remote options are also available for eligible positions.
