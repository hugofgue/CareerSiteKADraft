Engineering Services

Structural, mechanical, and electrical engineering, quantity surveying, and oil & gas. The core multidisciplinary engineering teams who turn designs into buildable, costable realities — from concept calculations to detailed MEP, BOQs, and shop drawings across buildings, industrial, and energy projects.

Construction Supervision

On-site oversight, works inspection, and quality assurance. Our resident engineers and inspectors make sure every project is built to the design, on schedule, and to international standards.

Project & Program Management

PMC services, PMO, project controls, contracts, stakeholder management, and HSE. We lead complex multi-billion-dollar programs from initiation through handover — including flagship engagements in Makkah, Madinah, Jeddah, Riyadh, and Khobar.

Planning & Urban Design

Urban planning and city and regional master planning. We shape how cities grow — from mixed-use districts and giga-projects to integrated regional development frameworks.

Digital & Technology

GSI and ESRI platforms, BIM and digital design management, application development, utility solutions, and digital product engineering. We build the geospatial systems, smart-utility tools, and BIM workflows that power our projects and clients' operations.

Environment & Sustainability

Sustainability advisory, environmental impact studies, water treatment, renewable energy, and QHSSE. We help clients meet net-zero, ESG, and regulatory targets across the region's most demanding infrastructure and development programs.

Facilities Management

Integrated facilities operations, asset performance, lifecycle maintenance, and workplace services — delivered as a dedicated K&A business line through our PMC engagements. Our FM specialists keep client assets operating reliably, safely, and efficiently from day one of handover.

Business Services

Finance, HR, Legal, IT, Communications, and Business Development. The corporate teams that keep a 6,500-strong, 30+ office firm running smoothly and growing across the region and beyond.

Infrastructure

Water supply and wastewater, stormwater and drainage, roads, highways and bridges, transport and traffic engineering, power transmission and generation, ICT networks, and airports. The civil and utility specialists behind the region's most critical networks.

Architecture & Interior Design

Concept design across residential, commercial, retail, healthcare, education, sports, hospitality, aviation terminals, landscape architecture, and interior design with FF&E. Our architects and designers shape landmark buildings and interiors across every sector.
